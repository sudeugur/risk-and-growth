# DeFi Lending Protocol: Credit Risk & VC Panel Engine
A protocol-based risk analysis and monitoring platform developed to help you make more informed decisions in the DeFi world.

**Risk & Growth** is a modern dashboard application that evaluates DeFi protocols based on risk scores, TVL values, category-based analyses, and key risk metrics.

## About the Project

DeFi protocols have many different risk factors, such as smart contract vulnerabilities, liquidity problems, oracle manipulations, and governance risks. Risk & Growth is designed to make these risks more understandable and trackable.

The platform analyzes protocols in DeFi categories such as lending, DEX, staking, yield, and provides the user with a simple, visual, and easily readable risk overview.

## Features

- Real-time risk monitoring interface
- Protocol-based risk scores
- TVL, category, chain, and 24-hour change indicators
- Smart Contract Risk analysis
- Liquidity Risk analysis
- Oracle Risk analysis
- Governance Risk analysis
- Low, medium, high, and critical risk levels
- Dashboard, protocol analytics, and risk framework sections
- Suitable structure for TR/EN language selection
- Ready-to-use interface for wallet connection

## Risk Assessment Model

Risk & Growth evaluates protocols based on four fundamental risk dimensions:

### Smart Contract Risk

Assessment is based on smart contract security, audit history, code complexity, upgrade mechanisms, and past security events.

### Liquidity Risk

Liquidity depth, pool stability, withdrawal mechanisms, and potential liquidity crunch scenarios are analyzed.

### Oracle Risk

Reliability of price data, oracle diversity, data currency, and resistance to manipulation are examined.

### Governance Risk

The governance structure, token distribution, concentration of voting power, timelock mechanisms, and multisig configurations are evaluated.
